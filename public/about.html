About EZPZ Windows

EZPZ Windows is a South Florida hurricane impact windows and doors specialist. We exist for one reason: to make buying impact windows as easy as it should be — real prices on screen in two minutes, no sales calls, no data selling, and Miami-Dade NOA approved products from the manufacturers that already dominate the South Florida market.

What we do

We supply and professionally install hurricane impact (HVHZ-rated) windows and doors for residential and small-commercial properties across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. Every product carries a current Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA), the strictest impact-resistance certification in the United States.

Our product catalog spans single hung windows, horizontal rollers, picture windows, geometric shapes, single and double entry doors, Bermuda doors, and 2/3/4-panel sliding glass doors in clear, tint, Lowe-366, or frosted laminated impact glass.
