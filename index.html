Frequently asked questions

How much do hurricane impact windows cost in Florida?

Hurricane impact windows in Florida typically cost $45 to $115 per square foot installed in 2026. A typical South Florida single-family home with 12 to 15 openings ranges from $14,000 to $42,000 for a full replacement.

What is Miami-Dade NOA, and why does it matter?

A Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) is the strictest impact-resistance certification in the United States. It is required throughout the Florida High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), which covers all of Miami-Dade and Broward counties. What Miami-Dade NOA means →

Impact windows or hurricane shutters — which should I choose?

Impact windows are always protected, add resale value, lower insurance, and need no setup before a storm. Shutters are cheaper upfront but require manual deployment, storage, and do nothing for noise, UV, or security between storms. See the full comparison →

Do impact windows actually reduce my homeowners insurance?

Yes — Florida law requires insurers to offer wind-mitigation discounts. After install, a licensed inspector issues an OIR-B1-1802 form documenting impact-rated openings; homeowners typically see a 10–45% reduction in the wind portion of their premium.
